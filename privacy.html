Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how AutoFlowCompliance (“we”) collects, uses and protects personal information when you use this website and our services.

Information we collect

We collect what you give us through our forms: your name, email address, business name, website address and any message you write. When you request a free audit report, we collect the URL to audit and the email address to send it to.

How forms are delivered

Our forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes the submission to email it to us. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may log technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

How we use your information

We use your information to reply to you, run the audit you asked for, send your report and quote, and deliver services you buy. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to deal with your request and any engagement that follows, and access is limited to the people doing that work.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. Email autoflowcompliance@outlook.com.
