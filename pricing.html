Pricing questions

Why do you cost more than an accessibility widget?

Widgets add a script on top of your site and charge monthly. They don't change the underlying code. We fix the code itself, test it by keyboard and screen reader, and document each change. That takes skilled time, which is what the fee covers.

What does “from” mean?

It's the starting price for a small site with a typical number of issues. After the audit we send a fixed quote. You'll know the exact cost before any work begins, and it won't change unless you add scope.

Are there ongoing fees?

No. Each engagement is a one-time fixed fee. If you later want a follow-up audit after a redesign or new content, we quote that separately.

How long does it take?

Audit report: within 24 hours of access. Full fix: typically 3–7 days. Full bundle: typically 5–10 days. Large or complex sites can take longer, and we'll tell you up front.

What if new issues appear after you finish?

Our report records the state of your site on the day we re-tested it. New content, plugins or theme changes can bring new issues, so we recommend a periodic re-audit if your site changes often.

Is your report a legal certificate?

No. It documents the technical work: the issues found, the changes made and the re-test results. You can share it with your lawyer, but it's not legal advice or a guarantee against claims.

How do I pay?

By invoice. 50% deposit before work starts, 50% on delivery.
