n8n lead intake: retries, dead letter, alerting

An 80-second silent screen recording with on-screen captions, made on a local n8n instance. The demo fires four requests at a webhook: a new lead, a duplicate resent in different casing, invalid input, and a simulated CRM outage.

  • Webhook → validation → idempotency check on the lowercased email → CRM write with Retry on Fail (3 tries, 2 s apart).
  • On a failed write: an error branch to a dead-letter Google Sheet step and a Slack alert, then a 202 to the caller.
  • A separate Error Trigger workflow reports any outright crash to Slack.

In this local demo the "CRM" is a public test endpoint (httpbin.org/post, and httpbin.org/status/503 to simulate an outage), and the Sheet and Slack credentials are placeholders, so those steps run but don't post anywhere.
