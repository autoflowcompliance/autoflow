Most websites have barriers that automated tools can detect.

Missing alt text, low-contrast text and unlabelled form fields lock out customers who use screen readers, magnification or a keyboard. They are also the issues demand letters cite.

Why it matters

The rules that apply to your website

A short, sourced summary. We are not a law firm, so speak to a lawyer about your own situation.

United States: ADA Title III

The Department of Justice says businesses open to the public must make their websites accessible to people with disabilities, and points to WCAG as helpful guidance.

DOJ, Guidance on Web Accessibility and the ADA (2022)

United Kingdom: Equality Act 2010

Service providers, online ones included, must make reasonable adjustments so disabled people are not put at a substantial disadvantage.

Equality Act 2010, legislation.gov.uk

Selling into the EU: the EAA

The European Accessibility Act applies from 28 June 2025 to many products and services sold in the EU, including e-commerce. Penalties are set by each member state.

Directive (EU) 2019/882, EUR-Lex

How it works

Audit, fix, evidence. In that order.

You see the problems and the price before you pay anything.

  1. 1

    We audit

    We run axe-core across your key templates, then check them by hand with a keyboard and a screen reader, because automated rules only catch part of WCAG.

  2. 2

    We fix the code

    We fix contrast, alt text, labels, focus order, headings and ARIA in your theme or codebase. No overlay script and no monthly fee.

  3. 3

    You get the evidence

    A remediation report lists every issue with its axe-core rule and WCAG criterion, what we changed, and a re-test showing the result.

Questions

Straight answers

Will this stop me being sued?

No one can honestly promise that. What we do is remove the barriers we find, re-test, and document the work so you have a clear record of what was fixed and when. For legal questions, speak to a lawyer.

What is in the free audit report?

We run axe-core on the page you send us and email you the issues it finds, grouped by rule and impact, with the WCAG criterion for each. It is automated only, and we say so in the report.

Do you need access to my site?

Not for the free report. For remediation we work in your theme or repository, usually through a staff account or a pull request, so you review every change.

How is the price set?

After the audit we send a fixed-price quote based on what we found. You pay a 50% deposit to start and the rest on delivery. See pricing.
