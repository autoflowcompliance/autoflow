What happens next

From request to report

  1. 1

    You send the URL

    The form sends your URL and email to our inbox. Nothing runs against your site until we start the audit.

  2. 2

    We run axe-core

    We load the page in a real browser and run the open-source axe-core engine against WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 A and AA rules.

  3. 3

    You get the report

    Within 24 hours, by email. If you want the issues fixed, reply and we'll send a fixed-price quote.

What an automated audit can and can't tell you

axe-core reliably finds issues such as missing alt text, low contrast and unlabelled fields, but no automated tool can test everything in WCAG 2.1 AA. Things like whether alt text is meaningful, or whether a checkout works by keyboard alone, need a person to check. Our paid audits add those manual checks. The free report covers automated results only and says so.
