About

A small specialist practice that fixes the code

Small businesses usually get two options: an audit-only PDF with nobody to do the fixes, or an overlay widget that doesn't change the code. We offer a third: audit, fix and document, for a fixed fee.

How we work

We're a small specialist practice serving businesses in the US and UK. Our audits are built on axe-core, the open-source accessibility engine maintained by Deque Systems, and our own scanner, AutoFlow, wraps it to produce issue-by-issue reports.

Automated tools are a starting point, not the finish line. So we also check pages by hand with a keyboard and a screen reader, fix the issues in your theme or codebase, then re-test and write down exactly what changed.

We use AI-assisted development tools to work quickly, and a person reviews every change before it reaches your site.

We audit first, quote second

You see what's wrong and the fixed price before you commit to anything.

We change the source

Fixes go into your HTML, CSS and templates, where they keep working without an overlay script.

We document everything

Every issue is logged with its axe-core rule, WCAG criterion, the change made and the re-test result.

Our commitments

What we won't claim

Accessibility is full of overpromising. Here is where we draw the line.

No lawsuit guarantees

No one can promise you won't receive a demand letter. We reduce barriers and document the work. For legal advice, speak to a lawyer.

No “100% compliant” badges

Conformance depends on content that changes over time. Our report records what we tested and when.

No automated-only verdicts

Automated rules cover only part of WCAG. Our reports say which checks were automated and which were done by hand.

US and UK, one standard

ADA Title III in the US, the Equality Act 2010 in the UK and the European Accessibility Act all point to the same technical baseline: WCAG. We work to WCAG 2.1 AA for every client.
