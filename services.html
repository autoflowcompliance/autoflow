Accessibility remediation

A WCAG 2.1 AA audit followed by fixes in your code. This is the core service for ADA Title III, Equality Act 2010 and EAA concerns.

  • axe-core audit of your key page templates
  • Manual keyboard and screen reader checks
  • Code-level fixes: contrast, alt text, labels, focus, headings, ARIA
  • Re-test after the fixes
  • Remediation report with before/after evidence per issue

GDPR & privacy review

For UK businesses and US companies with European customers. A practical review of what your website collects and how you describe it.

  • Review of the data your site's forms and scripts collect
  • Privacy policy reviewed and updated to match
  • Consent capture on forms
  • A simple process for handling data subject requests
  • Written summary of findings and changes

Cookie & consent setup

Most sites load analytics and marketing scripts before anyone agrees. We inventory them and put consent first.

  • Inventory of every cookie and third-party script
  • Accessible consent banner that blocks non-essential scripts until opt-in
  • Cookie policy drafted to match what the site actually does
  • Google Analytics consent configuration
